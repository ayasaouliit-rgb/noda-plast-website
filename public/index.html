About NODA PLAST

A flexible packaging partner built around your needs

NODA PLAST is part of AGRO FILM Group, a US$120 million flexible packaging group. We provide BOPP film solutions designed around the evolving requirements of flexible packaging customers.

Our manufacturing facility in Sétif, Algeria, operates an advanced 8.7-meter, five-layer BOPP production line, combining manufacturing capability with the flexibility to respond to different customer requirements.
